Bring your own scroller

One dataset map, mountMap() from @granum/core/map, beside three scrollers it does not own. Each passes a small MapTarget: its row count, the rows in view, a change signal, and a way to scroll to a row. No React on this page.

TanStack Virtual, 500,000 rows
