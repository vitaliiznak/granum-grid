# deepscroll launch film

A silent product walkthrough with real table interactions. The suggested voiceover below is a script for a later recording; no voice or music is generated by the capture command. Scene timing is configured in `src/scenes.ts`; the generated manifest and SRT record the final timing.

| Scene | Minimum | What viewers see | Suggested narration |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `hero` | 5 s | A short scroll around row 73.5 million | “Big tables need more than a scrollbar. They need a sense of place.” |
| `jump` | 6 s | Type 73,500,000 and jump to that exact address | “Go straight to the row you need, without losing the bigger picture.” |
| `overview` | 5 s | Switch from rows to a dataset-wide aggregate view | “Zoom out to see the shape of your data.” |
| `genome` | 5 s | Fly to the BRCA1 landmark in a generated genome | “Bring billions of addresses, your own cells, and meaningful landmarks.” |
| `logs` | 6 s | Search `status >= 500`, then inspect a match | “Let your provider search, and keep each match in context.” |
| `market` | 5 s | Jump to a named market event | “Turn events into places you can return to.” |
| `stars` | 5 s | Navigate to Sirius in a dark-themed catalog | “Use the same explorer with a completely different kind of table.” |
| `lens` | 5 s | Switch to Focus + context | “Keep the detail readable and the surrounding context close.” |
| `dynamic` | 5 s | Expand a note and measure its changing height | “Give longer records the room they need.” |
| `compare` | 6 s | Select two ranges and open linked comparison panes | “Compare two ranges. Keep exploring with deepscroll.” |

The default sequence has 53 seconds of planned scene time. Capture overhead may extend it slightly. Use each scene's `seconds` field to make a shorter or longer cut; keep enough time for the action and its outcome to read clearly.

For a shorter teaser, make a working copy of the scene list containing `hero`, `jump`, `genome`, `stars`, and `compare`, then export again. Single-scene MP4s are also useful for assembling a custom edit without changing the source order.

Keep the visible **Synthetic demo**, sampled-summary, and partial-search labels in the final crop. The film illustrates navigation and rendering; it does not establish real-data throughput or scientific validity. No media has been published by the export commands.
